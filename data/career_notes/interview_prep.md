# Ace the Job Interview

An interview is a two-way conversation. Preparation is key to standing out and showing culture fit.

## The STAR Method
When answering behavioral questions, always structure your answers using the STAR method:
* **Situation**: Describe the situation or context you were in. (15% of answer)
* **Task**: Explain the challenge or goal you needed to address. (15% of answer)
* **Action**: Detail the specific steps *you* took to solve it. Focus on your contribution. (50% of answer)
* **Result**: Highlight the outcome, using metrics if possible. (20% of answer)

## Typical Questions
1. **"Tell me about yourself"**: Connect your past experience directly to why you're excited about this specific role. Keep it under 2 minutes.
2. **"What is your greatest weakness?"**: Name a real area of development, and immediately describe what active measures you're taking to improve it (e.g. taking a course, reading books).
3. **"Describe a time you had a conflict with a team member"**: Focus on communication, empathy, compromise, and the successful resolution that benefited the project/business.

## Ask Intelligent Questions
At the end of the interview, always ask questions:
* "What does success look like in the first 90 days in this role?"
* "What is the biggest technical challenge the team is currently facing?"
* "How does the team handle learning and professional development?"
