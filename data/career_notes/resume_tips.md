# Resume Writing Best Practices

A great resume is your ticket to a job interview. It must highlight your impact, skills, and qualifications clearly.

## Action-Oriented Bullet Points
* Begin every experience bullet point with a strong action verb (e.g., "Led", "Developed", "Optimized", "Designed", "Initiated").
* Avoid passive phrases like "Responsible for" or "Duties included".
* Structure your bullet points using the **X-Y-Z formula**: Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y], by doing [Z].
* E.g., "Optimized database query response times by 35% (Y) through indexing and caching strategy implementation (Z), improving dashboard page load performance (X)."

## Quantifying Achievements
* Always search for ways to introduce metrics.
* Instead of: "Worked on customer service issues."
* Use: "Resolved over 150+ customer support tickets weekly, achieving a 94% satisfaction rating."

## Key Sections
1. **Header**: Contact info and LinkedIn.
2. **Professional Summary**: A 3-sentence summary of who you are and what value you bring.
3. **Core Skills**: Clean list of tech stack and domain expertise.
4. **Experience**: Reverse chronological list of roles.
5. **Education**: Degrees, years, and scores if high.
6. **Projects**: Showcase personal or open-source work if you have limited formal experience.
